About Imoratok Safaris

An authentic Ugandan ecotourism company launched by two outdoor enthusiasts dedicated to transformative travel, community empowerment, and the wilderness of East Africa.

Our Passion & Purpose

Born from a Genuine Love for Uganda

We are a Ugandan ecotourism adventure company launched by two Ugandan outdoor enthusiasts, adventurers, and friends, Emma Obyara and Paul Olweny, who shared a genuine passion for the natural beauty, wild places, and wildlife of Uganda. Emma and Paul also shared a simple dream and desire to share this natural kingdom with others and decided to start Imoratok Safaris to offer the best, most rewarding adventure safari expeditions around Uganda, with a focus on offering guests the most immersive, invigorating, and inspiring Uganda travel experiences.

Imoratok Safaris caters to individuals and small group sizes of between 4 and 18 guests in order to focus on delivering authentic and genuine life-altering adventure travel experiences in a unique way.

Choosing to travel with us will give you the best adventure travel experience of Uganda because of our local knowledge, expertise, and network with local leaders who are essential in sharing genuine local insights that enrich your experience while empowering local communities and helping conservation efforts.

We work with you to design not just a holiday, but an experience. Our job is our passion, designing bespoke, customized safaris to our beautiful and diverse country, Uganda.

Responsibility & Ethics

Experience Uganda Community Tourism

We strongly believe in leading the way to a brighter future because to whom much is given, much is expected, and with great adventure comes even greater responsibility. This is why we give back to the local communities we work with and contribute to wildlife conservation, environmental preservation projects, and a firm commitment to gender equality in local tourism by encouraging, mentoring, and training women to take an active role in the adventure tourism sector and in our business.

Known for a community-based lifestyle, it goes without saying that your safari to Africa will have community involvement. This may come in different forms, such as a planned visit to the community, cultural entertainment at the lodge, a stop by the roadside, or porter services offered at the park.

This is an initiative aimed at uplifting the community and, as a result, encouraging the conservation spirit in communities around the national parks. This initiative also goes a long way to reduce human-wildlife conflicts.

Expert Guides

Our guides are deeply knowledgeable in tourism, hospitality, regional history, and the distinct cultures of our destinations. Every guide is equipped with basic first aid skills and vehicle repair capabilities for total peace of mind.

Custom 4x4 Fleet

Our safaris feature custom-built 4x4 Land Cruisers and Jeeps with pop-up photographic roofs, driven by Uganda's finest guides. We specialize in tailor-made, customized, and luxury expeditions. If you can dream it, we make it happen.

Women in Tourism

We actively mentor and train Ugandan women as guides, logistics managers, and hospitality leaders, creating equitable livelihood opportunities across East Africa’s safari industry.

Where We Travel

Destinations Across East Africa

Uganda

The Pearl of Africa

Uganda, the Pearl of Africa as defined by Sir Winston Churchill in the 1930s (locally translated as "Uganda Enkkula"), is an emerald endowed with beautiful landscapes, culture, wildlife, and the most welcoming people. Bordered by Rwanda, D.R. Congo, Tanzania, Kenya, and South Sudan, it is a cradle of primates—home to Mountain Gorillas, Golden Monkeys, and Chimpanzees. In the Kazinga Channel, Uganda boasts the highest concentration of Hippos in Africa. Protected areas include 10 national parks and 6 wildlife reserves. Water bodies cover nearly one-third of the country, including Lake Victoria and the source of the River Nile in Jinja. Highlights include whitewater rafting, hiking Mt. Rwenzori, and scaling the Virunga volcanoes (Gahinga, Sabinyo, and Mufumbira).

Rwanda

The Land of a Thousand Hills

Rwanda, also known as the Land of a Thousand Hills, is decorated by stunning rolling hills and a border with Uganda and Congo formed by a chain of volcanoes forming scenic splendor like no other. Today, Rwanda is one of the premier primate tracking destinations and offers exceptional luxury eco-experiences. Volcanoes National Park offers gorilla tracking, golden monkey tracking, and volcano hiking. Nyungwe National Park is home to 13 primate species including chimpanzees, black-and-white colobus, and Sykes monkeys. Akagera National Park features savannah species including elephants, zebras, giraffes, buffalo, lions, leopards, and rhinos, making it an African Big Five habitat.

Kenya

The Pride of Africa

Kenya is the classic and most celebrated safari destination. On a contemporary safari, you can lose yourself for days, untroubled by crowds, communing with the bush and its inhabitants in an almost spiritual experience. The best time to visit Kenya is during the dry season from late June to October. The wildebeest migration usually reaches Masai Mara by August and remains until October before moving toward Serengeti in Tanzania. Wildlife viewing remains rewarding throughout the year across Kenya's diverse ecosystems.

Tanzania

The Land of Kilimanjaro

The sheer scale of Tanzania makes a single visit unforgettable. The annual wildebeest and zebra migration in the Serengeti National Park easily fills a week of your holiday. Climbing Kilimanjaro—Africa's highest free-standing mountain—is an iconic expedition. Other remote and fascinating parks include Tarangire, Katavi, Ruaha, and Selous. The Ngorongoro Crater is a must-see for its dramatic geology and dense wildlife, while the chimpanzee sanctuaries of Mahale in the west and the spice island of Zanzibar offer serene respite.
