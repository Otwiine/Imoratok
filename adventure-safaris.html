Adventure Safaris in Uganda

High-octane expeditions: White-water rafting on the Victoria Nile, equatorial glacier mountaineering in the Rwenzoris, Sipi Falls climbing, and Nile Perch angling.

Pure East African Adrenaline

Where Untamed Geography Meets Adventure

Uganda's dramatic physical landscape—bisected by the Great East African Rift Valley, crowned by snow-capped alpine mountains on the equator, and carved by the mighty Nile River—creates an unmatched playground for thrill-seekers.

In Jinja, the adventure capital of East Africa and the historic Source of the River Nile, guests conquer world-class Grade 5 white-water rapids and bungee jumps over the Nile. In the west, the Rwenzori Mountains ("Mountains of the Moon") rise to 5,109m at Margherita Peak, offering technical alpine trekking through glaciers and afro-montane vegetation.

For anglers, the swirling waters below Murchison Falls and Lake Victoria present world-class sport angling targeting monster Nile Perch weighing up to 100kg.

Bespoke Adventure Itineraries

Our specialized adventure expedition dossiers (including technical Rwenzori mountaineering circuits, Jinja Grade 5 white-water rafting, Mount Elgon Sipi hiking, and Murchison Nile sport angling) are customized directly by founders Emmanuel Obyara and Paul Olweny according to seasonal water levels, group fitness, and technical gear requirements.

Nile River Rafting (Jinja)

Grade 5 commercial rapids at the Source of the Nile, kayaking, river surfing, and quad biking along rural riverbanks.

Mountains of the Moon (Rwenzori)

Multi-day alpine trekking circuits through five distinct vegetation zones to permanent glaciers and Margherita Peak (5,109m).

Nile Perch Sport Angling

Catch-and-release heavy tackle sport fishing targeting monster Nile Perch below Murchison Falls and Lake Victoria.
