Cultural Safaris in Uganda

Profound, ethical cultural immersions connecting you with Uganda’s indigenous Karamojong pastoralists, Manyatta villages, Sipi coffee farmers, and Batwa forest heritage.

Living Heritage & Community Wisdom

Indigenous Traditions Across 7 National Parks

Uganda's cultural richness is unmatched. With over 56 distinct ethnic groups living across diverse biomes, our cultural safaris are built upon genuine local relationships, dignity, and direct community empowerment.

In the remote, sun-drenched Karamoja sub-region, we venture into traditional Manyattas (fenced mud-and-thatch homesteads), visit cattle corrals, explore the Moroto Museum, and visit Nakapelemoru Village—home to the Jie clan and the largest traditional settlement in all of East Africa with over 30,000 residents living according to ancestral values.

Combined with Mount Elgon Arabic coffee farming tours, cocoa farming in Bundibugyo, and the ancient forest traditions of the Batwa, our cultural itineraries weave human stories seamlessly into Uganda’s grand wildlife tapestry.

Flagship Grand Expedition

19 Days Wildlife and Culture Uganda Experience

This itinerary culminates most aspects of any Uganda wildlife experience into an epic 19-day safari combining 7 national parks and 1 game reserve: Mt. Elgon, Pian Upe, Kidepo Valley, Murchison Falls, Kibale Forest, Queen Elizabeth, Bwindi Impenetrable, and Lake Mburo.

19 Days / 18 Nights Entebbe • Sipi Falls • Karamoja • Kidepo • Murchison • Kibale • QENP • Bwindi • Mburo

Day 1: Arrival in Entebbe

Meet and greet by an Imoratok Safaris professional guide, transfer to your accommodation. Overnight and dinner at Airport Link Lodge (Full Board).

Day 2: Transfer to Sipi, Mount Elgon National Park

Transfer to Sipi Falls in Eastern Uganda via Jinja town (historic Source of the Nile River). Arrive at Sipi in the evening. Overnight and dinner at Sipi River Lodge (Full Board).

Day 3: Hike Mount Elgon & Arabic Coffee Farmer Experience

Hike to the beautiful Sipi Falls on Mount Elgon extinct volcano, admiring highland birdlife and afro-montane vegetation. In the afternoon, visit a local Arabic coffee farmer to learn traditional picking, drying, and roasting. Overnight and dinner at Sipi River Lodge (Full Board).

Day 4: Transfer to Moroto via Pian Upe Game Reserve

Transfer north to Moroto town with a game drive in Pian Upe Game Reserve searching for cheetahs, leopards, lions, giraffes, elands, ostriches, and secretary birds across the dry plains. Overnight and dinner at Karatunga Camp (Full Board).

Day 5: Karamojong Community Tour in Moroto & Moroto Museum

Immerse yourself in authentic Karamojong Manyatta villages (mud and grass-thatched huts) and cattle corrals. In the afternoon, visit the Moroto Museum to understand regional cultural heritage. Overnight and dinner at Karatunga Camp (Full Board).

Day 6: Transfer to Kidepo Valley NP via Nakapelemoru Village

Drive through Matheniko and Bokora wildlife reserves to Kotido and visit Nakapelemoru Village—the largest traditional settlement in East Africa with over 30,000 Jie clan residents. Proceed to Kidepo Valley NP. Overnight at Kidepo Savannah Lodge (Full Board).

Day 7: Full Day Wildlife Viewing in Kidepo Valley National Park

Early morning and afternoon game drives through the untamed Narus Valley and Kidepo plains: lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants, buffalos, and ostriches. Overnight at Kidepo Savannah Lodge (Full Board).

Day 8: Transfer to Murchison Falls National Park

Overland scenic drive west across northern Uganda to Murchison Falls National Park, checking in at Murchison River Lodge (Full Board).

Day 9: Savannah Game Drive & River Nile Boat Cruise to Murchison Falls

Morning savanna search for Big Four game, followed by an afternoon boat cruise to the splashing base of Murchison Falls with dense hippo and crocodile pods. Overnight at Murchison River Lodge (Full Board).

Day 10: Murchison Falls at Leisure / Delta Boat Cruise

A relaxing day to photograph, review field journals, or opt for a morning delta boat cruise searching for the rare Shoebill Stork. Overnight at Murchison River Lodge (Full Board).

Day 11: Transfer to Fort Portal & Kibale Forest National Park

Drive south along the Albertine escarpment past tea plantations to Kibale Forest NP. Overnight at Chimpanzee Guest House Lodge (Full Board).

Day 12: Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale & Bigodi Swamp/Village Walk

Morning chimpanzee tracking in the dense canopy of Kibale Forest with UWA rangers, followed by an afternoon guided walk through Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary observing up to 9 primate species. Overnight at Chimpanzee Guest House Lodge (Full Board).

Day 13: Bundibugyo Cocoa Farmers & Transfer to Queen Elizabeth NP

Visit Bundibugyo at the foot of Mount Rwenzori to tour local cocoa farming cooperatives, then cross the equator into Queen Elizabeth National Park. Overnight at Enjojo Lodge (Full Board).

Day 14: Full Day Game Viewing in Ishasha (Tree-Climbing Lions)

Game drives across the Ishasha sector searching for famous tree-climbing lions resting in fig trees. Proceed south into the Kigezi highlands to Lake Mutanda Resort (Full Board).

Day 15: Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Scenic drive through the winding mountain roads of Kigezi ("Switzerland of Uganda") into the ancient rainforest of Bwindi. Overnight at Lake Mutanda Resort (Full Board).

Day 16: Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

The pinnacle encounter: trek into the rainforest with UWA trackers to spend one full hour in the presence of wild mountain gorillas. Official trekking certificate awarded upon return. Overnight at Lake Mutanda Resort (Full Board).

Day 17: Bwindi Impenetrable NP at Leisure & Community Reflection

A leisurely day to reflect, visit surrounding mountain craft cooperatives, or relax overlooking Lake Mutanda. Overnight at Lake Mutanda Resort (Full Board).

Day 18: Transfer to Lake Mburo National Park

Drive to Lake Mburo National Park with game viewing en route for impalas, zebras, and elands, with an optional night safari. Overnight at Rwakobo Rock Lodge (Full Board).

Day 19: Return Drive to Kampala / Entebbe

Waking up at leisure for breakfast, drive back to Kampala/Entebbe with equator crossings and craft stops, transferring to Entebbe Airport for evening departure.
