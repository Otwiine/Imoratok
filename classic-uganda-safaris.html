Classic Uganda Safaris

Legendary Big Five game drives, thundering Nile river cruises, tree-climbing lions, on-foot rhino tracking, and night safaris.

Uganda's Premier Savannas

Untamed Wildlife, Rivers & Open Horizons

Our classic wildlife safaris are carefully designed to showcase Uganda’s finest national parks at unhurried pacing. From the thunderous cleft of Murchison Falls where the entire Nile plunges 43 meters, to the rich savanna plains and hippo-filled waters of Queen Elizabeth National Park, and the acacia woodland of Lake Mburo National Park.

Whether planning a quick weekend getaway from Kampala or a multi-day Big Five exploration with on-foot rhino tracking at Ziwa Sanctuary and chimpanzee tracking in Budongo Forest, every trip is conducted in private 4WD safari jeeps with pop-up photographic roofs driven by accredited USAGA guides.

Signature Expedition

5 Days Best of Murchison Falls National Park

Rhino Tracking • Chimpanzee Tracking • Top of the Falls Hike • Nile Boat Cruise • Savanna Game Viewing

5 Days / 4 Nights Entebbe → Ziwa Rhinos → Murchison Falls → Budongo → Entebbe

Day 1: Transfer to Murchison Falls NP via Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary

Depart after early morning breakfast heading north to Murchison Falls National Park with an en-route visit to the 7,000-hectare Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary. Experience an on-foot walking safari approaching wild southern white rhinos at close range with an experienced ranger. After lunch, proceed to Murchison Falls National Park and check in.

Accommodation: Murchison River Lodge, thatched safari tent (Full Board).

Days 2 & 3: Full Day Savannah Game Drives & Nile River Boat Cruise

Early morning wildlife searches across the untamed northern savannahs in search of lions, leopards, elephant herds, buffalos, Rothschild giraffes, Uganda kobs, and oribis. Return for hot lunch, followed by a 3-hour afternoon boat cruise on the Victoria Nile to the base of Murchison Falls with dense hippo and Nile crocodile populations and abundant waterbirds.

Accommodation: Murchison River Lodge, thatched safari tent (Full Board).

Day 4: Morning Chimpanzee Tracking in Budongo Forest & Evening Game Drive

Head into Budongo Forest for an unforgettable chimpanzee tracking excursion, following an expert tracker into the primary forest to observe wild chimpanzees during their daily routines of grooming, vocal displays, and foraging. Return for lunch, followed by an evening sunset game drive.

Accommodation: Murchison River Lodge, thatched safari tent (Full Board).

Day 5: Top of Murchison Falls Viewing & Return to Entebbe/Kampala

After breakfast, drive to the dramatic Top of the Falls where the River Nile explodes violently through a 7-meter gorge before plunging 43m below. Enjoy packed lunch en route, arriving back in Entebbe/Kampala in the afternoon.

Classic Weekend Getaway

3 Days Ultimate Wildlife: Queen Elizabeth National Park

Designed especially for individuals, couples, or groups wishing to take a short holiday to experience Kasenyi plains wildlife and the Kazinga Channel boat cruise.

3 Days / 2 Nights Kampala → Queen Elizabeth NP → Kampala

Day 1: Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

Depart early morning from Kampala with scenic stopovers for lunch, equator photos, and long drive stretches. Arrive in the mid-afternoon, check in, and embark on an evening game drive to wind up the day. Overnight and dinner at Simba Safari Lodge / Fort Portal.

Day 2: Kasenyi Plains Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Early morning game drive into the Kasenyi plains in search of lions, leopards, elephants, buffalos, and large numbers of savannah bird species. Return for lunch, followed by a 2-hour afternoon boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel shores filled with hippos and elephants.

Day 3: Transfer to Kampala / Entebbe

Morning breakfast at leisure and return drive to Kampala/Entebbe with en-route lunch and scenic stops.

Express Wildlife Safari

2 Days Lake Mburo National Park Ultimate Wildlife Experience

Game Viewing • Walking Safari on Foot • Night Safari • Boat Cruise

2 Days / 1 Night Kampala/Entebbe → Lake Mburo → Kampala/Entebbe

Day 1: Transfer to Lake Mburo, Boat Cruise & Night Game Drive

Transfer 4–5 hours from Kampala with an equator photoshoot stop. Arrive at Rwakobo Rock Lodge for lunch, followed by an afternoon boat ride on Lake Mburo observing abundant birdlife and hippos. After dinner, embark on an exciting night drive in search of nocturnal wildlife: leopards, bush babies, civet cats, and aardvarks. Dinner & overnight at Rwakobo Rock Lodge.

Day 2: Morning Walking Safari on Foot & Return to Kampala/Entebbe

Experience a 2-hour morning walking safari led by an armed Uganda Wildlife Authority site guide, encountering zebras, impalas, elands, and giraffes at close range on foot. Return to the lodge for refreshment, then transfer back to Kampala/Entebbe.
