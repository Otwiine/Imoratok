About Imoratok Safaris

Imoratok Safaris was founded in 2020 by two Ugandan friends, Emmanuel Obyara and Paul Olweny, who share over 15 years of combined experience working within Uganda and across East African countries. Their deep passion for wildlife conservation and cultural heritage inspired them to create a safari company that offers truly authentic African experiences.

The name “Imoratok” is Emmanuel's clan name. It is derived from two words: Moru, meaning mountain, and Tok, meaning star — referring specifically to Venus, the planet. Just as Venus shines brightest among the stars above the mountains, Imoratok Safaris aims to be the guiding light for travellers seeking unforgettable adventures in East Africa.

From the misty mountains of Bwindi to the vast savannas of Kidepo Valley, we craft journeys that connect you with the raw beauty and rich cultures of Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda. Every safari is a personal story waiting to unfold.

Helpful Safari Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to help you prepare for your gorilla trek and African wildlife journey.

How much does a Uganda gorilla trekking permit cost in 2026?

A standard foreign non-resident gorilla trekking permit in Uganda is $800 per person for Bwindi Impenetrable and Mgahinga Gorilla National Parks. For visitors seeking four full hours with a semi-habituated gorilla family, the Gorilla Habituation permit is $1,500.

Permits must be booked in advance as daily availability is strictly capped to protect gorilla health. Imoratok Safaris purchases and secures official permits directly from the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) on your behalf.

When is the best time for wildlife safaris in Uganda?

The best months for wildlife viewing and gorilla trekking are Uganda's dry seasons: June to August and December to February. Forest trails are drier and easier to hike, while savannah animals congregate around permanent water holes in Queen Elizabeth and Murchison Falls.

However, Uganda is a year-round destination with warm tropical weather; the green seasons (March–May and October–November) offer lush landscapes, lower lodge rates, and exceptional bird watching.

What physical fitness is required for mountain gorilla trekking?

Gorilla trekking involves hiking through untracked rainforest terrain at altitudes between 4,000 and 7,500 feet. Treks typically last between 1 and 6 hours depending on the troop's movement.

A moderate fitness level is recommended. Local porters are available for hire at park headquarters ($15–$20) to carry backpacks and provide physical assistance on steeper paths—a service we strongly recommend that also directly empowers surrounding mountain communities.

Why choose a native-owned safari operator like Imoratok?

Traveling with native Ugandan guides ensures unmatched bushcraft, deep understanding of wildlife behavior, and genuine connections with local communities. Having operated in East Africa for over 15 years, founders Emmanuel and Paul provide intimate insights foreign tour brokers cannot match.

Booking with Imoratok also ensures that your tourism investment directly supports Ugandan guides, porters, women-led craft cooperatives, and local conservation initiatives.
