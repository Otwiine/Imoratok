About Imoratok

Imoratok Safaris was founded in 2020 by two Ugandan friends, Emmanuel Obyara and Paul Olweny, who share over 15 years of combined experience working within Uganda and across East African countries. Their deep passion for wildlife conservation and cultural heritage inspired them to create a safari company that offers truly authentic African experiences.

The name “Imoratok” is Emmanuel's clan name. It is derived from two words: Moru, meaning mountain, and Tok, meaning star — referring specifically to Venus, the planet. Just as Venus shines brightest among the stars above the mountains, Imoratok Safaris aims to be the guiding light for travellers seeking unforgettable adventures in East Africa.

From the misty mountains of Bwindi to the vast savannas of Kidepo Valley, we craft journeys that connect you with the raw beauty and rich cultures of Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda. Every safari is a personal story waiting to unfold.

Meet the Founders

Emmanuel Obyara

Co-Founder & Lead Guide

Emmanuel brings over 15 years of expertise in wildlife guiding and safari logistics across East Africa. His clan name, Imoratok, inspires the company's identity — a beacon of excellence in safari experiences. Emmanuel's intimate knowledge of Uganda's national parks and his dedication to sustainable tourism make every journey extraordinary.

Paul Olweny

Co-Founder & Operations Director

Paul manages the operational backbone of Imoratok Safaris, ensuring every trip runs seamlessly from booking to farewell. With deep roots in Ugandan culture and extensive travel industry experience, Paul builds relationships with local communities that enrich every traveller's experience with authentic cultural encounters.
