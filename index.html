Roots & Heritage

The Guiding Star of East Africa

Imoratok Safaris was founded in 2020 by two Ugandan friends, Emmanuel Obyara and Paul Olweny, who share over 15 years of combined experience working within Uganda and across East African countries. Their deep passion for wildlife conservation and cultural heritage inspired them to create a safari company that offers truly authentic African experiences.

The name “Imoratok” is Emmanuel's clan name. It is derived from two words: Moru, meaning mountain, and Tok, meaning star — referring specifically to Venus, the planet. Just as Venus shines brightest among the stars above the mountains, Imoratok Safaris aims to be the guiding light for travellers seeking unforgettable adventures in East Africa.

From the misty mountains of Bwindi to the vast savannas of Kidepo Valley, we craft journeys that connect you with the raw beauty and rich cultures of Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda. Every safari is a personal story waiting to unfold.

Safaris

Birding Safaris

Uganda is home to over 1,000 bird species. Our birding safaris take you through wetlands, forests, and savannas to spot the elusive Shoebill, Great Blue Turaco, African Green Broadbill, and hundreds more.

Ultimate Wildlife Safaris

Encounter the Big Five and beyond across Uganda's premier national parks. From tree-climbing lions in Ishasha to herds of elephants in Queen Elizabeth NP, experience East Africa's most iconic wildlife.

Primate Safaris

Track mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and chimpanzees in Kibale. Uganda is one of the best places on Earth for primate encounters, home to 13 primate species.

Sport Fishing Safaris

Cast your line into the legendary waters of the Nile and Lake Victoria. Pursue the mighty Nile Perch and Tilapia while surrounded by stunning landscapes and abundant birdlife.

Cultural Safaris

Immerse yourself in Uganda's rich cultural tapestry. Visit the Batwa pygmies, the Karamojong warriors, and royal kingdoms. Experience traditional dance, crafts, and ceremonies that have endured for centuries.
